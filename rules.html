Investor World of Business rules

Introduction

Investor World of Business is a Slovak game similar to monopoly. The game is played on a monopoly-style board, with cards representing each property. Players buy up properties, can build houses, and try to bankrupt other players who step on their properties.

This document describes an augmented ruleset to make this game more engaging.

Starting money

Players start the game with 400k spread out across varying bank notes.

(200k is too little, 300k prevents any investing, 500k is too much)

Investing

The game allows players to store money in the bank. Every time the player passes the START field, this money appreciates by 10%.

Riziko zisk

A number of the cards give players a 50% or a 100% discount on payments. In the game, a card can give a player up to 10 tokens. We change this slightly:

  • If the card specifies up to 5 tokens, give 1 token.
  • Above 5, give 2 tokens.

These tokens can be used to reduce:

  • Rent payments
  • Pokuta payments
  • Riziko zisk payments
  • Bank interest rates for 1 loan

They CANNOT be used to reduce prices of properties and branches.

Loans

SELLING PROPERTIES IS NOT ALLOWED. Players may take a loan from the bank and put up any number of properties as collateral, once per round. The amount they get per property is specified in HYPOTEKA. The player receives this money immediately. Every time a player passes the START field, an interest rate of 10% is applied to the amount. The player can only repay the loan when passing the start field.

If a player needs to take more than one loan per round, they go bankrupt and exit the game.

Players are allowed to sell properties to each other and transfer the corresponding loans.

Jail

A player can step on a jail field. By doing this they forfeit the right to play for two rounds. They also do not collect any rent payments during this time.

Winning conditions

The game ends when all but one player are bankrupt, or when all players decide to end the game. In the latter case, all players’ properties are sold to the bank for the amount specified by HYPOTEKA. The player with the most money wins.
